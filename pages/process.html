Step-by-Step Inheritance Process (California, via Trust)

What actually happens when the trust distributes the house and cash — ordered, with official sources for every procedural step. Deadlines are legal deadlines, flagged where verified.

Phase 1 — Before death (prep checklist)

  1. Confirm the property is titled into the revocable living trust. A trust only avoids probate for assets actually funded into it (deed recorded naming the trust). VERIFIED guidance CA Courts — Living Trusts
  2. Keep the pour-over will as a backstop for anything not funded into the trust. VERIFIED same source.
  3. Record the deed of trust with the county recorder if not already recorded (a trust document alone does not move title). VERIFIED guidance CA Courts — Wills & Trusts
  4. Estate-tax planning checkpoint: if combined taxable estate approaches $15M/person (2026), consider lifetime gifting ($19,000/yr per donee verified) and portability planning with a CPA/attorney. VERIFIED thresholds IRS advice = professional, not this site

Phase 2 — At death (first 30 days)

  1. Obtain multiple certified death certificates (counties, insurers, banks, assessor all ask). VERIFIED practice CA Courts
  2. Successor trustee steps in — no probate court needed for trust-titled assets; the trustee needs typically the trust document + death certificate. VERIFIED same source.
  3. Locate all assets: trust property, POD/TOD accounts, retirement/beneficiary designations (these pass outside the trust automatically). VERIFIED CA Courts
  4. Notify insurers of the death and continue coverage on both properties (internal task — no official deadline; flag for follow-up).

Phase 3 — Property tax & assessor actions (deadlines)

  1. File change-in-ownership / death notice with the county assessor. Transfers at death are a change of ownership for Prop 13 purposes. SF: Assessor-Recorder; Santa Clara: Assessor. VERIFIED requirement SF TTC (reassessment on change of ownership) · BOE LTA 2025/023
  2. Decide the Prop 19 path for Property A within statutory deadlines:
    • BOE-19-P (parent-child exclusion claim) — within 3 years of death/transfer, or before transfer to a third party, whichever is earlier. VERIFIED BOE Prop 19 checklist
    • Homeowners' exemption (BOE-100-H / BOE-266) — within 1 year of transfer; requires the heir to occupy as principal residence. VERIFIED same.
    If the heir will NOT occupy: skip exclusion, expect full FMV reassessment (see Property Tax page).
  3. Watch for the supplemental assessment notice after reassessment; appeal window runs from the notice date (SF supplemental process VERIFIED at SF TTC; assessment-appeal deadlines: BOE Pub 30). exact window per notice — flag for calendar
  4. Record death of joint tenant / trustee affidavits if applicable (BOE-502-D within 150 days for some filings). VERIFIED form BOE forms index confirm applicability internally

Phase 4 — Federal & state tax filings

  1. Form 706 (federal estate return): required only if gross estate + adjusted taxable gifts > $15,000,000 (2026), or to elect portability of a deceased spouse's unused exclusion. Due 9 months after death. VERIFIED IRS FAQ · i706
  2. California estate tax return: NOT required for deaths on/after 2005-01-01. VERIFIED SCO
  3. Document the stepped-up basis for the house and investments: date-of-death appraised value (no 706 filed → appraisal is the evidence). VERIFIED IRS Pub 551
  4. Trust/estate income tax (Form 1041/541) if the trust earns income before distribution (e.g., rent collected). VERIFIED requirement exists IRS Form 1041 · FTB fiduciary estimated tax applicability depends on trust terms — flag

Phase 5 — The decision (use the analysis page)

  1. Choose live / rent / sell for Property A using the verified levers on Live · Rent · Sell.
  2. If selling: stepped-up basis → gain = post-death appreciation only; federal LTCG 0/15/20% + NIIT 3.8% + CA ordinary rates ≤13.3% apply to that slice; ~6% selling costs assumed (flagged). rates VERIFIED Rev. Proc. 2025-32
  3. If renting: AB 1482 + Cupertino ordinance rules apply (see Rental page); rental income is ordinary income; depreciation deductions apply — confirm with Pub 527 flag.
  4. If living in it: file BOE-19-P + homeowner's exemption within deadlines (Phase 3); keep occupancy continuous — moving out later can end the exclusion (BOE LTA 2006/17 VERIFIED).

Phase 6 — Close the loop

  1. Update insurance policies to new owner/trustee names (internal).
  2. Re-check tax bills on both properties against this site's tables; update user-provided rows and clear flags where the official bill resolves them (internal).
