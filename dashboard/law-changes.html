Why this matters to the decision

Of everything on this page, only one change would reverse the recommendation: a repeal or substantial softening of Proposition 19's inherited-property rules. That is the change worth tracking every year. Three attempts have failed, which is evidence — not proof — that it is unlikely in the near term.
