How to read the verification column

Verified means the value was read directly off the official document linked in the same row. Owner input means it came from private records and cannot be checked publicly. Unverified means the value is deliberately blank because no official source could be found — it was never inferred or filled in. See Methodology.

Anonymisation

ZIP codes and round-number market estimates are retained because rate research requires a ZIP and because neither is individually identifying. Everything that could identify a person, a parcel, or a trust is excluded. See Privacy & Anonymisation.
