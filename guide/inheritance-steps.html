Inheritance process, step by step

StepActionDetailOfficial source
1Confirm the decedent's principal residence status and the trust termsRevocable trust transfer is excluded, but CIO occurs when the trust becomes irrevocable unless the trustor remains the sole present beneficiary. Distribution provisions (sprinkle powers) can affect exclusions.S04, S35
2Obtain the parcel's tax bills and assessed value from the County AssessorReconcile client-provided history with bills (see irregularity IR01). Do not rely on unverified history.S06, S07
3Decide whether the heir will move in (Prop 19 family home) or notMove-in within one year and file homeowners' exemption within one year. Property B: only an owner-occupied unit qualifies.S02, S03, S05
4File the BOE-19-P (parent-child claim) within 3 years of transfer and before any third-party transferFailure to file on time can affect the exclusion and assessment timing.S02
5Estimate estate size against the federal basic exclusion ($15,000,000 in 2026)Federal return threshold is based on the estate's total value. Two properties at client values are well below the 2026 figure; other assets matter.S09, S10
6Heir's basis steps up to fair market value at deathGain is measured from the date-of-death value. Keep appraisals and records.S12
7Hold, rent, or sell decisionSale proceeds: county transfer tax $1.10 per $1,000 (city taxes unverified). Home-sale exclusion under section 121 may apply if the heir lives in the home 2 of 5 years.S13, S29
8Plan for California income tax on income from inherited assetsInheritance itself is not CA income. Rental income and investment gains are taxable. No state estate or inheritance tax.S15, S19
9Plan for the 3.8% NIIT if investment or rental income exceeds thresholdsSingle threshold $200,000 (MAGI).S14
These steps are a planning checklist, not legal advice. Probate or trust administration steps are not covered here and need an estate attorney.
