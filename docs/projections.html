Recommendation

Sell the Cupertino house at inheritance and reinvest. Renting it after the Prop 19 reassessment produces a net loss; living in it costs ~$79K/yr; selling produces roughly $300,000/yr of after-tax investment income by 2045 (moderate scenario) with no property tax drag.
