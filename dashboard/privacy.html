Privacy & anonymisation

Personal information privacy is the top priority for this project. The rules applied to this repository and to the published site.

Nothing on the published site identifies a person, a parcel, or a trust. No name, street address, assessor parcel number, trust name, account number, date of birth or date of death appears in any file in this repository, and none appears on the site.

The rules

DataTreatment
Names of owners, heirs, trustees, beneficiariesNever recorded. Not needed for any calculation.
Street addressesNever recorded. Not needed for any calculation.
Assessor parcel numbersNever recorded.
Trust names and datesNever recorded.
Tenant identities, leases, tenancy datesNever recorded. Rent is stored as an aggregate monthly figure.
ZIP codesNever recorded. Omitted from every file, matching the convention the other sessions in this repository adopted. Research is scoped by city and county, which is sufficient.
Round-number market value estimatesRetained. Not identifying on their own, and required for every projection.
Property tax and assessment historyRetained as owner input. These are private records; they are published only to the extent needed to show the flagged irregularities.

Naming convention

Properties are referred to as Property A (Cupertino, Santa Clara County, CA — the property expected to be inherited) and Property B (San Francisco, CA — the property currently owned and occupied). The letters are used consistently across every page, every data file, and every research note.

Internal versus published

The distinction that matters: personal information discovered while researching is for internal use only. It may exist in a private note or in a conversation, but it must never be written into a published page or a committed file. If a lookup requires an address — for example, the City of Cupertino water service provider map — do it in a browser and record only the result, never the address.

If something leaks

If you spot personal information anywhere in this repository or on the site, treat it as an incident: remove it, and note the removal in COORDINATION.md. Because GitHub Pages serves a public site from a public repository, anything committed is effectively published.

Not tax, legal, or investment advice. This is a research dashboard. Every verified value links to the official document it was read from; anything that could not be confirmed from an official source is blank and listed on Flagged for Review.
