Solar: what the official rule says versus the client claim

The CPUC states the residential fixed charge is about $24.15 per month for the standard rate. Solar credits are reported not to offset it. The client reports a net credit of $10–$20 per month, which is consistent only if the credit is on energy charges alone, or if the fixed charge is not on the bill. Both cases are modeled, and the base case uses the stricter one ($9.15 net monthly cost).

Resolve with 12 months of bills and the interconnection date (see FL-05).
