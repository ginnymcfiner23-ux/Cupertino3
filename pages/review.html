Source irregularities to keep in mind

  • Older California tax webpages can contain obsolete statements about the former federal state-death-tax credit. This dashboard does not rely on those old figures for current federal thresholds.
  • A market-value estimate is not the assessor’s taxable value; a tax bill is not necessarily equal to 1% of market value. Prop. 13, local tax-rate areas, direct assessments, exemptions, and supplemental bills matter.
  • The Santa Clara County Prop. 19 explainer appears to have legacy/ambiguous text; the BOE’s more recent notice and forms are cited and the inconsistency is listed above.
  • California’s 2025–2027 Prop. 19 exclusion amount is time-bounded and must not be projected as the amount applicable in 2040–2050.
  • Premium surveys are comparisons for hypothetical homes. No insurance dollar figure is asserted for either property.
  • Future 2040–2050 market, tax, and expense numbers are not filled in because the official data and assumptions needed to support them do not exist yet.

This page is deliberately transparent: unverified values are blank, not treated as zero, and not exposed from the private request.
