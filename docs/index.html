What this site answers

Inheriting Property A (Cupertino) through [Trust] around 2040-2050, compared with holding Property B (San Francisco). It covers property tax, assessment history, insurance, water, electricity, maintenance, federal and California law, the inheritance threshold, and the live / rent / sell decision.

  • Federal estate tax: the 2026 exclusion is $15,000,000 per person (verified, IRS). Projected to $19,792,181 in 2040 and $24,126,559 in 2050 at a 2% inflation assumption.
  • California: no estate or inheritance tax (historic sources; current confirmation pending).
  • Prop 19 is the main driver for Property A. In the 2045 base case, property tax is about $52,264 a year if the beneficiary occupies the home as a principal residence, and about $72,583 a year if not.
  • Live / rent / sell (base case, 2045 inheritance, 10-year horizon, 3% appreciation, 4% investment return): keeping the home beats selling only if its rent (or imputed rent) is above about $11,024 a month with occupancy, or $12,647 without. With a 7% return, no rent level in the search range makes keeping better.
  • Property B returns about 3.27% net of costs in 2026 at the user's rent, before income tax and before appreciation.
