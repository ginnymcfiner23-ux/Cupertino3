What this means for the trust

  • An estate with a gross value under the exclusion owes no federal estate tax. Form 706 is required above the filing threshold, and it can also be filed to elect portability of a deceased spouse's unused exclusion (IRS, verified).
  • The threshold applies to the whole estate, not to Property A alone. Property A's value ($3,100,000 in 2026 per user estimate) is only part of the estate.
  • California has no estate or inheritance tax (historic sources; pending current confirmation). The state threshold is therefore not a constraint.
  • The top federal estate tax rate is 40% (partially verified; flagged).
