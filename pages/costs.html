Operating costs

Category • Property operating costs

Cost projections need bills and quotes

There is no official, reliable citywide premium or household utility figure that can be substituted for a property-specific quote or bill. This section shows what is verifiable, what must stay blank, and how to assemble the annual carrying cost.

No household cost figures are published. Home-specific water, electricity, insurance, rental receipts, tax bills, repair history, and property estimates are private and were not matched to official parcel or account records. The unknown values are blank by design, not zero.

Cupertino-area primary-residence insurance—Request an owner-occupied quote using replacement cost, construction, age, roof, deductible, liability, coverage, and property-specific underwriting. Compare CDI’s hypothetical premium survey cautiously; it is not an individual quote.CDI 2026 homeowners comparison tool
San Francisco rental / landlord insurance—Request a landlord/dwelling or suitable multi-unit policy quote with actual number of units, tenant occupancy, liability, loss-of-rents, replacement cost, claims, and coverage limits. An owner-occupied homeowners quote may not cover rental use.CDI residential insurance guide
Water and sewer—Use actual utility bills, meter size, consumption, sewer flow factor, and current rate schedule. San Francisco PUC rates are published by the utility; Cupertino-area service provider and tariff must be confirmed from the actual bill.SFPUC water/sewer rates
Electricity / solar credits—Use twelve months of utility statements and the current interconnection/net-billing tariff. Solar export credits and true-up charges depend on system, tariff, usage, and utility account.California Public Utilities Commission: solar in California
Property tax—Use actual secured and supplemental bills, official taxable value, exemptions, direct assessments, and tax-rate area. Do not estimate from current sale value alone.San Francisco annual assessed-value notice
Repairs and maintenance—Build from inspection, invoices, age/condition of roof, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, drainage, pest, solar, and major appliances. A documented reserve is a planning assumption, not an official amount.California DOI: insurance scope and replacement cost
Rental income and expenses—Use executed leases and rent ledger; subtract vacancy, management, insurance, property tax, utilities, maintenance, repairs/capital improvements, and financing. Tax depreciation is distinct from cash expense.IRS Publication 527
Sale and reinvestment taxes—Use inherited basis, selling costs, prior rental depreciation, residence use, and investment income/tax assumptions; get a CPA calculation before treating sale proceeds as investable cash.IRS Publication 523

Insurance research result: California’s Department of Insurance states that premiums vary by location, local fire protection, building age/construction, deductible, discounts, and coverage. Its comparison tool uses hypothetical risks; it says an exact premium requires an insurer/agent quote. It is therefore not defensible to publish a “Cupertino” or “Outer Sunset” premium without underwriting details.
