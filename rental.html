The unresolved coverage question

San Francisco's 1.6% cap applies only to covered units, and coverage was not confirmed from the Rent Ordinance in this pass. It is commonly tied to a certificate of occupancy on or before 1979-06-13, with single-family homes and condos exempt under Costa-Hawkins. If Property S is not covered, the statewide AB 1482 cap governs instead, and that is materially higher. Over twenty years the difference between 1.6% and roughly 8.8% annual rent growth is very large. Get the certificate-of-occupancy date. See F7.
