Projections

Category • Projections

Project the rules; leave unknown figures blank.

Long-range values through 2040–2050 cannot be verified from ZIP codes. This page gives the lawful formulas, the required inputs, and an auditable projection structure without inventing home values, taxes, insurance, or inflation.

Why no dollar forecast is published: no parcel/APN, verified enrolled value, tax-rate area, tax bill, future transfer date, insurer quote, utility usage/rate schedule, repair history, or market-growth assumption is available for public verification. User-provided financial/asset figures are withheld for privacy. A numerical forecast would falsely imply certainty.
