Bottom line for this estate

California will collect nothing on the inheritance. If the total estate (houses + money + other assets) stays under the federal exemption for the year of death (~$15M/person in 2026, indexed), no federal estate tax is owed at all. If it exceeds the exemption, 40% is owed only on the excess, paid by the estate.

What this means for these two houses

  • Cupertino (rental): inherited → reassessed to full market value. Property tax rises from ~$7,207 to ~$72,247/yr by 2045 (moderate scenario).
  • SF (principal residence): inherited → factored base + $1M-capped exclusion if the heir moves in within 1 year and files the exemption. New taxable value ≈ market value − allowance → ~$24,009/yr by 2045 (moderate scenario).
  • Either arrangement (SF as family home vs Cupertino as family home) yields nearly the same combined property tax (~$96,256 vs $95,976/yr in 2045) — the capped exclusion simply shifts between the two houses.
