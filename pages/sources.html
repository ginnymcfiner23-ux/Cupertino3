Links below point to federal, California, county, city, and state insurance/utility sources. For case-specific statements, see the source column on the applicable table row.

Verification boundary: Laws and published reference values were checked against linked official sources as of 6 November 2026. No parcel-specific records, quotes, or utility accounts were accessed. Links are for manual review; household figures remain private and withheld.
