California estate tax
$0
Pick-up tax that computes to zero (R&T Code 13302)

Nothing is owed to California for receiving the inheritance. Revenue and Taxation Code section 13302 imposes a California estate tax only to the extent of the federal Credit for State Death Taxes. Congress repealed that credit, so the California tax computes to zero. California Legislative Information, R&T Code 13302

Statutory text: California Revenue and Taxation Code section 13302

Notwithstanding the provisions of Section 13301, whenever a federal estate tax is payable to the United States, there is hereby imposed a California estate tax equal to the portion, if any, of the maximum allowable amount of the Credit for State Death Taxes, allowable under the applicable federal estate tax law, which is attributable to property located in the State of California. However, in no event shall the estate tax hereby imposed result in a total death tax liability to the State of California and the United States in excess of the death tax liability to the United States which would result if this section were not in effect.

California Legislative Information · Part 8 heading: "Prohibition of Gift and Death Taxes"

Other taxes that can still bite

Capital gains on a later sale

Basis resets to fair market value at death, so the gain accrued during the decedent's life is never taxed. Gain accrued after the date of death is taxable. IRS Pub 551

Inherited retirement accounts

IRAs and 401(k)s are not covered by the step-up rule. Distributions to a non-spouse beneficiary are ordinary income, generally within ten years. Flagged as not researched in this pass.

Generation-skipping transfer tax

The GST exemption matches the estate exclusion at $15,000,000 for 2026, but it is not portable between spouses and must be allocated. IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32
