Executive summary

Sell at inheritance is the strongest modeled plan for both properties

Two California properties may pass through a trust around 2040–2050. Under current law the state takes no inheritance tax, and federal estate tax applies only above a large threshold. The main choices are to sell, keep and rent, or keep and live in the home. In the base case, selling and investing the proceeds beats keeping either property in every inheritance year and stress case tested. The gap is wide for Property A and narrower for Property B in the low-growth case.
