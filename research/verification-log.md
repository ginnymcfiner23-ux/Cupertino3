# Verification log

Every value published with a **Verified** badge on this site was read directly from the official
document listed here, during the build. This file records what was checked, from what document, and
what was deliberately left blank.

Method: the primary document was fetched and the value read off it. Secondary sources (law-firm
blogs, rate aggregators, news coverage) were used only to locate primary documents — never to
populate a value. Where a secondary source suggested a figure that could not be traced to a primary
document, the value is blank and the item is on the site's Flagged for Review page.

## Irregularities found in the supplied data

**F1 (high).** Property S: owner-supplied 2025 tax of $2,439 on a 2025 assessment of $114,248
implies an effective rate of 2.13%. San Francisco's verified FY2025-26 rate is 1.18268325%, which
would produce about $1,351. The gap runs through every year supplied (1.73%–2.10%).

**F2 (medium).** Property C: owner-supplied 2025 tax of $7,207 on a 2025 assessment of $539,743
implies an effective rate of 1.335%. Secondary sources describe Santa Clara County totals as
generally 1.12%–1.24%. The implied rate declines over the series (1.41% in 2016 → 1.34% in 2025),
which is consistent with bonds being retired, but the level is above the quoted range.

Neither was "corrected", because correcting either would mean guessing. Both are published as-is
with the flag attached.
