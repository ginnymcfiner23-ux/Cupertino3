## Relevance to this estate

- Cash/investments inherited in trust are included in the gross estate at date-of-death value (excepted: life insurance/retirement with named beneficiaries pass outside).
- Simplified federal estimate for large taxable estates: **tax ≈ $5,945,800 + 40% × (taxable estate − $15,000,000)** (model simplification — ignores deductions; flagged on site).
- If total estate (both properties + cash) is below $15M per person / $30M per couple with portability, **no federal estate tax return is required** unless electing portability.
