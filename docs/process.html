Inheritance and transfer process

Steps and deadlines are taken from the cited sources. A qualified professional should confirm each one for the specific trust and beneficiary.

StepWhenActionSource
1At date of deathThe transfer is treated as occurring on the date of death. Obtain a date-of-death fair market value appraisal, which sets the income-tax basis.S04 S10
2Within 9 months of deathFile Form 706 if the gross estate exceeds $15,000,000 (2026). Extension: Form 4768 (6 months). Payment is also due within 9 months. Filing on time is required to elect portability.S09 S14
3Before occupancyDecide whether a beneficiary will occupy Property A as principal residence. Only that arrangement can keep the base value plus the add-on. Rentals, vacation homes and LLC-held property do not qualify.S03
4Within 1 year of transferThe occupying beneficiary files the homeowners' exemption claim (BOE-266) with the county assessor. A claim filed by February 15 gets the full exemption for that year.S03 S05
5Within 3 years of transfer, or before any third-party transferFile the Prop 19 change-in-ownership statement (BOE-19-P).S03 S04
6OngoingKeep the home as the beneficiary's principal residence. If it stops being the principal residence, it is reassessed at the next lien date (value = market value at inheritance, adjusted yearly by the inflation factor).S04
7Each yearCheck the assessment against the BOE factor (2% cap; 2026-27 factor 1.02) and the value-limit add-on ($1,044,586 through Feb 15, 2027; adjusted every two years).S01 S02
8At transfer and each yearInsurance: confirm owner-occupied dwelling coverage. FAIR Plan dwelling is an option if the broker's search finds no private market (1-4 unit owner-occupied and rental dwellings are eligible).S20 S21
9At transferTransfer utility accounts. Check the solar interconnection date and the tariff (NEM 2.0 terms run 20 years from interconnection).S22 S23
10If the property is soldIncome tax on the gain: federal long-term rates, NIIT (3.8%) above the threshold, and California tax at ordinary rates (no separate capital-gains rate). Basis = date-of-death value (plus improvements).S08 S12 S16 S10
