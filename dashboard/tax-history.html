Verification notes

Assessment growth is consistent with Prop 13

Almost every year in both series grows at exactly +2%, which matches the Prop 13 maximum. The Santa Clara County Assessor confirms the 2% cap governed for 2025-26 because California CPI was 2.514%, above the cap.

Property B implied rate is roughly double San Francisco's official rate

The owner's figures imply about 2.13% for 2025. San Francisco's verified combined secured rate for FY2025-26 is 1.18268325%. The gap is present in every year supplied. It is most likely direct charges or special assessments bundled into the tax figure, or an incomplete assessment figure. Resolve before relying on any Property B cost projection.
