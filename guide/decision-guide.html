How to decide

  1. If the heir wants to live in Property A, the Prop 19 family-home treatment is worth confirming before the move-in date. The one-year move-in and exemption filing are strict (steps).
  2. If living in A is not a goal, the base-case projections favour selling and reinvesting, since the reassessed tax and carrying cost are high relative to the income the proceeds could earn (model return, not a forecast).
  3. For B, the main choice is between keeping the rental (reassessed tax, pre-tax NOI about $45,812/yr at 2045) and selling. An heir who occupies one unit reduces the tax (illustrative $21,910/yr), but that is not verified for this building.
  4. Check the figures in Flagged for Review before deciding. The tax history does not reconcile to official rates.
