What the numbers say

  • Occupancy matters. With occupancy the break-even rent is $11,024/month; without it, $12,647/month. Reassessment without occupancy adds about $20,319 a year in property tax in 2045 (base case).
  • The return assumption decides it. At a 7% return, selling and investing wins at any rent in the search range. At 4% (2045 inheritance, occupied), the break-even is $12,103 a month at 2% appreciation, $11,024 at 3%, and $3,926 at 5%.
  • Maintenance is a large cost. The 0.5% of value assumption is about $27,179 a year in 2045. Replace it with the actual figure before deciding.
  • Property B yields 3.27% in 2026 before income tax and before appreciation. With 3% appreciation the total pre-tax return is roughly 6.3%, below a 7% investment return and above a 4% return. No tax or mortgage is included.

Recommendation (within the stated assumptions)

On these assumptions, selling and investing is the default unless verified rent for Property A (or a real living benefit that the family would otherwise pay for) exceeds the break-even above. If the beneficiary will live in the home, the occupancy condition for Prop 19 must be met and documented, because it lowers the break-even by about $1,623 a month in 2045 terms.

Before deciding, verify: the market values (appraisals), the solar interconnection date and tariff, the maintenance cost, the Cupertino rent, the beneficiary's residency, and the investment return the family can actually obtain. This is not financial or legal advice.
