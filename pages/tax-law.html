What is changing / what remains unknown?

Enacted federal change: Public Law 119-21 raised the 2026 federal basic exclusion to $15 million and provides inflation adjustment after 2026. Future indexed figures will be released in later years.

Current California property-tax mechanism: Prop. 19’s exclusion is periodically adjusted; the 2025–2027 amount should not be carried forward as if fixed.

California proposal watched: SB 284 (2025–2026) proposed additional limited exclusions for eligible transfers among family members and a revised start date for the one-year residence period in certain probate cases. Its official status record reports that it was held in committee and under submission on July 14, 2025; it must not be treated as enacted law without a later chaptered bill.

California enacted narrow change: SB 293 (Chapter 539, 2025) created a limited claim extension for specific 2025 wildfire/probate cases. It does not broadly change ordinary parent-child inheritance eligibility.

2040–2050: No reliable statement can be made today about future amendments, tax thresholds, property-tax exclusions, insurer availability, utility rates, or local assessments. The site deliberately gives no invented future law or value forecast.

Monitoring: Re-check IRS estate/gift tax updates, California BOE Prop. 19 guidance, the applicable county assessor, and current legislative status before trust revisions and immediately after death.

Tax warning: Estate tax is generally an estate-level tax; the beneficiary’s income tax, estate/trust fiduciary income tax, property tax reassessment, and capital-gains tax are distinct analyses. Coordinate an estate attorney and CPA who can inspect the trust, all assets, lifetime gifts, and ownership structure.
